# Changelog

## v11 — Metropolis / Passenger expansion
- Added player and AI landing lights.
- Added expandable navigation map.
- Increased visual airborne travel speed without raising displayed airspeed.
- Added paid Passenger Mode using Sky Credits.
- Expanded each country from several towns to a nation-scale metropolitan network.
- Added much denser buildings, skyscraper districts, road links, moving traffic and city illumination.

## v11.1 Traffic & Lighting
- Cut gate NPC density roughly in half again.
- Reduced airborne NPC traffic from six to three.
- Removed the aircraft pre-seeded at hold short so the player gets runway access much sooner.
- Increased turnaround delays so traffic does not immediately refill.
- Player pushback increased from 3.2 to 8.5 simulation units.
- AI pushback increased from 4.2 to 8.0.
- Rebuilt landing lights as bright bulbs with layered transparent halos and a softer forward beam instead of solid-looking blocks.

## v11.2 — Rapid Ops
- NPC taxi, lineup, takeoff, approach, landing rollout and taxi-in are substantially faster.
- Primary-runway separation reduced to 2 seconds; parallel runway separation also shortened.
- NPC finals are shorter and flown faster to keep traffic moving.
- Player arrivals receive priority; Northpoint can reassign the player to runway 36L when 36R is busy.
- Northpoint automatically switches a waiting player to 36L after about 25 seconds when available.
- Target player landing-clearance wait is under one minute.

## v11.2 Dev Credits
- Developer wallet enabled for Passenger Mode testing.
- Sky Credits display as ∞.
- Passenger tickets still show their fare, but do not reduce the test wallet.
